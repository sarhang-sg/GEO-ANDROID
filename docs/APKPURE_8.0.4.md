## What’s new (English)

NAV KURD 8.0.4 adds reliable GPS recovery after background use, a redesigned
multilingual weather widget with real day/night, season, rain, snow, hail,
storm, dust, wind, heat and cold states, daily weather notifications, and
update alerts only for older installations. Map layers no longer let broad GPS
or route lines cover place icons. Device-aware performance, offline storage,
downloads, permissions, loading, sign-in, diagnostics, privacy pages and the
interactive tutorial were refined for a smoother Android experience.
